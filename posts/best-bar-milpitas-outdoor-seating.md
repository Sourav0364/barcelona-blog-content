## Decide what “outdoor seating” means for your visit

- **Setting:** Are you looking for an open-air patio, a covered area, or simply a seat outside? Ask the business to describe the current arrangement.
- **Weather:** If shade, rain cover, or heating matters, confirm what is available for the date and time you plan to visit.
- **Group size:** If several people are coming, ask whether the outdoor area can accommodate your group and whether an advance arrangement is needed.
- **Food and drinks:** Check whether the menu and beverage options you want are available for the seating area and time. Do not assume the same options apply everywhere.
