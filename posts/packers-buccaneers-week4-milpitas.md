## What is confirmed about Tampa Bay’s quarterback

The Buccaneers’ [Week 4 game preview](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/2026-game-preview-packers-buccaneers-week-4) says Daniels will make his NFL starting debut against Green Bay. The team says Mayfield is beginning rehab for a dislocated right thumb and is expected to miss at least three games. The [NFL’s report](https://www.nfl.com/news/buccaneers-qb-baker-mayfield-thumb-out-at-least-three-weeks-jalon-daniels) also confirms that Daniels is starting in Mayfield’s place. Tampa Bay’s [Oct. 1 injury report](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/buccaneers-packers-injury-report-oct-1-week-4-2026) lists Mayfield as not participating in practice because of the right-thumb injury.

That is current team and league reporting, not a prediction about the game’s result. It is also the most useful distinction for anyone following the Packers–Buccaneers matchup: the Sunday Week 4 game features a new starting quarterback for Tampa Bay.
