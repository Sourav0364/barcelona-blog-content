## Compare delivery details before ordering

A useful pizza-delivery comparison looks beyond the restaurant name. Ask or check for current information about:

1. **Menu and format:** Is the pizza you want currently available, and what size or format is offered?
2. **Delivery area:** Does the restaurant deliver to your exact address today?
3. **Timing:** What is the current estimated delivery window, and could busy periods affect it?
4. **Fees and minimums:** Are there delivery fees, order minimums, or other charges? Check the final total before submitting.
5. **Ordering method:** Is the order placed directly with the restaurant or through a third-party service, and who can answer questions about changes?
6. **Ingredients:** If an ingredient, allergy, or dietary need matters, ask the restaurant what it can verify about the current item and preparation.
7. **Handoff:** Confirm any delivery instructions and how the order will be left or handed over.

These questions make “best” a matter of fit—menu, distance, timing, and terms—not an unsupported ranking.
