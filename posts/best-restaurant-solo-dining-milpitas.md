- **Seating:** If you prefer a table, counter, or bar seat, ask whether that option is currently available. A venue photo does not establish its present layout or seating policy.

- **Setting:** Consider whether the venue’s current atmosphere suits the kind of solo visit you want. Do not assume that a restaurant is quiet or lively based only on its category or pictures.
