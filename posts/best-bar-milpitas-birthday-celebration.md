## Decide what kind of birthday plan you want

A birthday gathering can mean a casual round of drinks, a shared meal, or a longer group outing. Before comparing Milpitas bars, make a short list of what your group needs:

- **Group size:** How many people are likely to attend, and will the number change?
- **Food and drinks:** Does the group want food as well as drinks? Ask about the current menu and any dietary questions.
- **Setting:** Would a regular bar-and-restaurant setting suit everyone, or does your plan depend on a separate room or a quieter area?
- **Timing:** Does the date or time affect the plan? Confirm current hours and any arrangements directly.
- **Budget:** Ask about current prices, deposits, minimums, or other charges if they matter; do not rely on an old post or an assumption.
- **Special requests:** If you have a cake, decorations, or another birthday request, ask whether the venue permits or supports it before you make plans.

These details help you compare the actual fit instead of relying only on a rating or a “best bar” list position.
