When an NFL game is tied after regulation, what happens next? Searches for **NFL overtime rules 2026** are especially useful when a close game reaches the extra period: fans want to know whether both teams get the ball, how long overtime lasts, and whether a regular-season game can still end in a tie.

The key points for the 2026 regular season are straightforward: overtime is one 10-minute period, both teams have an opportunity to possess the ball, and a game still tied when the period expires ends as a tie. The NFL changed the regular-season possession format beginning in 2025, bringing it in line with the postseason approach that had been used since 2022. Details differ between the regular season and playoffs, so check the current rules if you are following a particular game.

## NFL overtime rules in 2026 at a glance

For a regular-season NFL game that is tied after regulation, the 2026 overtime format has these main features:

- Overtime starts with a coin toss to determine which team chooses whether to receive or kick off.
- Both teams have an opportunity to possess the ball during overtime.
- The regular-season overtime period lasts 10 minutes.
- Each team gets two timeouts for the overtime period.
- If the score remains tied when the 10-minute period ends, the regular-season game is recorded as a tie.

The rules change how fans should think about the opening possession. A team scoring first in overtime does not necessarily end a regular-season game immediately; the opponent has an opportunity to answer. The exact sequence and outcome depend on what happens on each possession and on the game clock.

## What happens after regulation ends in a tie?

If the score is tied after four quarters, the game moves to overtime under the NFL’s overtime procedure. A coin toss gives one team the choice of receiving or kicking off. The teams then play under the overtime rules rather than beginning another ordinary quarter of regulation.

The receiving team’s first drive is no longer the only possession that matters. Both teams have an opportunity to get the ball. That difference is important for fans who remember the earlier regular-season format, when an opening-drive touchdown could end the game before the other team had a chance to respond.

If the teams have each had an opportunity to possess the ball and one is ahead, that team wins. If the score remains level after both have had their first possession, the game continues under the overtime scoring rules; in the regular season, the clock remains a limit on how long the period can last. The 10-minute period can expire with the game still tied.

## Does the first team to score win in NFL overtime?

Not automatically. Under the current regular-season format, the opponent gets an opportunity to possess the ball even if the team with the opening possession scores a touchdown. The first score may put one team ahead, but it does not by itself guarantee that the game is over.

That is a common point of confusion for anyone who remembers older regular-season overtime. The NFL extended the both-teams-have-a-possession approach to regular-season games beginning with the 2025 season. The change followed the format already used in playoff overtime.

For a live game, follow the possession, score, and remaining time together. A scoreboard showing one team ahead in overtime does not tell the whole story if the other team has not yet had its opportunity or if time remains in the period.

## How long is regular-season NFL overtime?

A regular-season NFL overtime period is **10 minutes**. That does not mean every overtime game lasts exactly 10 minutes: the game can end earlier once the rules determine a winner. But if the period reaches its limit with the score still tied, the game ends as a tie.

Teams receive two timeouts each for the overtime period, according to current season explainers. Timeouts, possession changes, scoring plays, and reviews can all affect how much clock remains. For a game-specific question, use the official game clock and current rules rather than assuming overtime lasts a fixed amount of real-world time.

## Can an NFL regular-season game end in a tie?

Yes. If a regular-season NFL game remains tied when the single 10-minute overtime period expires, the result is a tie. Overtime is not an unlimited extra period during the regular season.

That result is different from playoff football. In the postseason, the game must produce a winner, so overtime continues until one team advances. Fans comparing an NFL Sunday game with a postseason game should not assume that the regular-season clock limit works the same way in the playoffs.

## A simple way to follow an NFL overtime game

When a game reaches overtime, use this quick checklist:

1. **Confirm the game is tied after regulation.** The overtime procedure applies only after the regulation score is level.
2. **Watch the coin toss and choice.** The coin toss determines which team chooses whether to receive or kick.
3. **Track each team’s possession.** Under the current format, both teams have an opportunity to possess the ball.
4. **Follow the score and clock together.** A lead after one possession may not mean the game is over, and the regular-season overtime period has a 10-minute limit.
5. **Know whether it is regular season or postseason.** A regular-season game may end in a tie; a playoff game must determine a winner.
6. **Use current official information.** Rules and schedules should be checked against current NFL sources, especially when explaining a live or upcoming game.
