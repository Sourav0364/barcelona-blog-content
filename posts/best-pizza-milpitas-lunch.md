1. **Hours for your date:** Is the restaurant open at the time you plan to arrive? Confirm directly, especially if your lunch window is short.
2. **Current lunch menu:** Is the pizza or format you want available during that time? A dinner menu, older photo, or general listing may not answer that.
3. **Order timing:** Ask about current preparation or pickup timing if you need to return by a certain time. Do not treat an online estimate as a guarantee.
4. **Ordering method:** Confirm whether the restaurant currently supports the option you need, such as ordering at the venue or pickup. Do not assume a service from a search result.

6. **Location:** Check the complete address and route from your actual starting point, not only the city shown in a result.
