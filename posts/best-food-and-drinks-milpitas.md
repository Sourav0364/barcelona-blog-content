## Compare the whole visit, not just one search result

Local restaurant searches may return review sites, directories, and broad lists. Before choosing, decide what “best” means for this particular outing:

- **Food style:** Are you looking for a familiar cuisine, a fusion concept, or food designed for sharing?
- **Drink plan:** Do you want craft cocktails, another beverage style, or options for a mixed group?
- **Timing:** Is a weekday afternoon visit relevant, or should you confirm other service times directly?
- **Location:** Does the address work for your plans in Milpitas, around Calaveras Boulevard, or from San Jose?
- **Details that matter:** Confirm current menu choices, ingredients, dietary needs, and availability with the business.

These checks make a broad search more useful without relying on an unsupported “best of” label.
