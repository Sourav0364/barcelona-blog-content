If you are hunting for the best happy hour in Milpitas, start on West Calaveras Boulevard. Barcelona Restaurant & Bar pairs discounted drinks with genuinely good food, five days a week.

Happy hour runs Monday through Friday, 3–6 PM at the bar — the perfect window to unwind after work or warm up before dinner.

## What is on offer during happy hour
Barcelona keeps the deals simple and the flavors bold:

- Discounted draft beer and house cocktails
- Sharable tapas straight from the charcoal kitchen
- Sangria by the glass and the pitcher
- Every match on the big screens

## Why locals pick Barcelona
It is not just about cheap drinks. The bar blends Indian fire, Spanish soul and Bay Area energy, so even the happy hour bites taste like a night out.
