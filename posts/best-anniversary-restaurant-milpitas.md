## Decide what “best fit” means for two people

Before comparing restaurants, talk through the kind of evening you both want. The right checklist may be different for a quiet dinner, a shared-plate meal, or a lively night with drinks.

- **Setting:** Would you prefer a low-key room or a more energetic atmosphere? Ask the restaurant about the current setting rather than inferring it from photos.
- **Food:** Are you looking for a particular cuisine, shareable plates, or individual orders? Verify specific menu items and ingredients directly.
- **Drinks:** Decide whether cocktails are part of the plan, and confirm what is currently available.
- **Timing:** Check the current operating schedule for your date. Do not infer evening hours from a search result or a weekday happy-hour window.
- **Location:** Confirm the full street address and route, especially when a result is described as “near” Milpitas rather than located in the city.

A fine-dining or romantic category can help shape a shortlist, but it does not establish a venue’s sound level, menu, seating, availability, or fit for your celebration.
