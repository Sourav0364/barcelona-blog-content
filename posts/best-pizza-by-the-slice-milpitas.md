## Start with the kind of slice you want

“By the slice” can mean different things from one restaurant to another. Before choosing, consider whether you have a particular crust style in mind, whether you want one slice or several, and whether everyone in your group wants the same kind. When an online menu is not clear, contact the restaurant and ask how slices are sold that day.

## Compare what a slice means at each place

A fair comparison goes beyond a listing’s star rating or the word “slice.” Check these details:

- **Serving size:** Ask how large a slice is and whether it is sold individually.
- **Style:** Confirm the crust and preparation style if you have a preference.
- **Availability:** Check whether slices are offered at your intended time or only by request.
- **Order format:** Ask whether you can combine different varieties, if that matters to your group.
- **Pickup details:** Verify current hours, wait time, and ordering options directly.
- **Dietary needs:** Ask about ingredients and preparation, including cross-contact concerns, before ordering.

This makes it easier to compare like with like. A whole pie, a single warmed slice, and a multi-slice order can serve different needs, so confirm the format instead of assuming that every pizza listing offers the same choices.
