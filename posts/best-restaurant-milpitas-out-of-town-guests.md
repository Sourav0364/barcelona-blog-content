## Start with your guests’ plans

Before searching, ask what would make the meal comfortable and enjoyable for your visitors. A few practical questions can narrow the options:

- **What kind of meal are you planning?** A quick stop and a longer sit-down meal call for different timing and location checks.
- **What food preferences should you account for?** Ask guests about cuisines, ingredients, and dietary needs rather than guessing from a restaurant’s name or photos.
- **What atmosphere do they want?** Some people prefer a lively bar-and-restaurant setting; others may want something quieter. Confirm the current environment if it matters.
- **Where will everyone be coming from?** Use the full street address to compare the route, not just a “near Milpitas” label.
- **Are drinks or a game part of the plan?** Check beverage availability or ask whether a particular game will be shown; general descriptions do not confirm current details.

This approach makes a broad search more useful than relying on a list position or a single review.
