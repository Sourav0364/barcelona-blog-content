## Identify what you want to watch

Before choosing a bar, note the matchup and kickoff time from an official schedule or broadcaster. College football weekends can include games at overlapping times, so check that the exact game you care about is available at the venue. If sound matters, ask whether it can be turned on for your game; do not assume that a screen showing sports means the audio will be available.

If you are meeting friends, decide whether everyone wants to follow one matchup or keep up with several scores. Then check the route, current operating hours, and how much time you want to spend there. A little planning makes it easier to compare local options on the details that actually affect your outing.
