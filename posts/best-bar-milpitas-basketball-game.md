## Confirm the game, not just the screens

A listing that mentions sports or screens is a useful starting point, not confirmation of a specific broadcast. Before choosing a spot for a basketball outing, identify the game and its start time, then ask the venue directly:

- Will this exact basketball game be available on a screen?
- Is audio available, and can you confirm whether it will be on?
- What time should your group arrive, and are current hours suitable?
- Are food and drinks available at the time you plan to visit?
- If you have a group or a seating preference, can the venue accommodate it?

Screens, sound, game access, seating, and current operating hours are separate questions. A little confirmation can save a trip based on an assumption.
