## Check what “vegan” means for your order

A listing tagged “vegan pizza” is a useful starting point, but it may not answer every ingredient or preparation question. Before choosing a place, ask about the items that matter to you:

- **Crust and sauce:** Confirm the current ingredients rather than assuming a standard recipe is suitable.
- **Cheese and toppings:** Ask what plant-based choices, if any, are currently available. Do not infer them from an old menu photo.
- **Preparation:** If avoiding contact with animal-derived ingredients is important, ask how the restaurant prepares the order and whether it can accommodate that request.
- **Dietary needs:** Explain any allergy or dietary restriction directly and ask the restaurant what it can verify. A “vegan” label alone is not an allergy or cross-contact guarantee.
- **Current availability:** Check whether the specific item is being served on the day and at the time you plan to order.

These checks help distinguish a general vegan-friendly tag from information about the exact pizza and preparation you need.
