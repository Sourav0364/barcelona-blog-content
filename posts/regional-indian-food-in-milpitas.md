Regional Indian food and Indian–Spanish fusion are related searches, but they describe different dining expectations. A regional search often begins with a specific tradition or flavor profile. A fusion search begins with curiosity about how Indian flavors interact with Spanish-inspired presentation, shareable formats, or a restaurant-and-bar setting.

Neither approach should be assumed from a business name or a photograph. If your group has a firm regional preference, ask the restaurant whether the current menu reflects that region.
