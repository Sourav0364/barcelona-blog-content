## How many teams make the NFL playoffs?

The AFC and NFC each send **seven teams** to the postseason: four division winners and three wild-card teams. The four division winners are seeded first through fourth in their conference; the three wild cards take seeds fifth through seventh. A wild-card berth goes to a team that did not win its division but earns one of the conference’s remaining playoff places.

## The NFL playoff bracket, round by round

### 1. The top seed in each conference gets a bye

The No. 1 seed in the AFC and the No. 1 seed in the NFC skip the opening round and advance directly to the Divisional Round. Under the current 14-team format, each conference has one first-round bye.

### 2. Six Wild Card games decide who advances

The other six teams in each conference play three single-elimination games. In each conference, the initial Wild Card matchups are:

- **No. 2 seed vs. No. 7 seed**
- **No. 3 seed vs. No. 6 seed**
- **No. 4 seed vs. No. 5 seed**

The higher seed hosts each of those games. A win advances a team; a loss ends its postseason.

### 3. The bracket reseeds for the Divisional Round

The No. 1 seed joins the winners of the Wild Card games. The highest remaining seed faces the lowest remaining seed in that conference, so the No. 1 seed plays the lowest-seeded Wild Card survivor. The other two remaining teams play each other, with the higher seed hosting.

That reseeding means the Divisional Round matchups are not simply fixed by the original Wild Card bracket. The winners of both Divisional games in each conference then advance to that conference’s championship game.

### 4. Conference champions meet in the Super Bowl

The AFC Championship Game and NFC Championship Game each produce a conference champion. Those two teams meet in the Super Bowl, the final game of the NFL postseason. The playoff games are single-elimination: one loss means elimination.

## NFL playoff format FAQ

### Does every NFL playoff team get a first-round bye?

No. Under the current 14-team format, only the No. 1 seed in each conference receives a bye. The other six AFC teams and six NFC teams play in the Wild Card Round.

### How are NFL Wild Card games matched up?

In each conference, the No. 2 seed hosts No. 7, No. 3 hosts No. 6, and No. 4 hosts No. 5. The No. 1 seed enters in the Divisional Round.

### Does the NFL reseed after the Wild Card Round?

Yes. The highest seed remaining in a conference faces its lowest remaining seed in the Divisional Round. The other two remaining teams meet in the other game, hosted by the higher seed.
