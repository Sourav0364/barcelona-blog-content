**What is the NFL two-minute warning?** It is an automatic official timeout that occurs at the end of the last play that began before the game clock reaches the two-minute mark. The NFL’s official glossary says the warning occurs only in the second and fourth quarters.

## What the NFL two-minute warning means

The warning is an automatic game stoppage, not a timeout requested by one of the teams. The official timeout comes at the end of the last play that started before the game clock reaches two minutes. That detail is why the stoppage is not necessarily a whistle at the exact instant the clock display changes to 2:00.

The [NFL Football Operations glossary](https://operations.nfl.com/rules-officiating/nfl-football-basics/football-terms) defines the timing, and the [2026 NFL Rulebook](https://operations.nfl.com/rules-officiating/2026-nfl-rulebook) is the current official rules reference. The key point for fans is simple: the two-minute warning is an automatic official timeout in the second and fourth quarters.

## When does the two-minute warning happen?

- **Second quarter:** The warning is the automatic timeout near the end of the first half; its precise timing follows the end-of-play definition above.
- **Fourth quarter:** The same clock rule applies late in regulation.
- **First and third quarters:** The NFL glossary says the two-minute warning occurs only in quarters two and four.

The warning marks a late-half point on the game clock; it does not predict the score or guarantee a result. It helps viewers orient themselves as they follow the action.

## What should viewers watch around the warning?

When the stoppage happens, check the score, quarter, down and distance, possession, and displayed time before drawing conclusions about the next play. The warning itself does not guarantee a particular strategy or result. In a close NFL game, the following plays may matter, but the teams’ choices depend on the live situation.

### In which quarters does the NFL two-minute warning occur?

The NFL glossary says it occurs only in the second and fourth quarters.
