## What to confirm before choosing

“Happy hour” does not tell you by itself which items are included, what they cost, or whether the schedule applies on a particular date. Before you head out, confirm:

1. The exact day and time of the happy-hour window.
2. Which food or drink options, if any, are included at that time.
3. Current prices and any conditions that affect your order.
4. Whether the venue’s setting and location fit your plans.
5. Any menu detail your group needs to know before arriving.

For a same-day plan, confirm directly with the restaurant rather than relying on an old listing or assuming the offer is unchanged.
