## Start with the people and the plan

A graduation dinner can bring together guests with different schedules and preferences. Before comparing restaurants, agree on a few basics:

- **Who is joining?** Get an approximate headcount before asking about seating. Do not assume a venue can accommodate a group of any size.
- **What kind of meal does the graduate want?** Decide whether the group hopes to share food, order individually, or keep the meal simple. Check the current menu rather than assuming a particular dish is available.
- **When will everyone be free?** If the ceremony or travel is part of the day, check its timing first. Then confirm restaurant hours and food availability for the time you have in mind.
- **What does the group need?** Gather dietary questions and practical requirements before calling. Ask the restaurant directly about ingredients and preparation if those details matter.
- **Where is everyone coming from?** Confirm the restaurant’s address and compare the route with the ceremony location and guests’ plans.

These details make a broad search for a **graduation dinner in Milpitas** more useful than choosing from a list based only on a label or photo.

## Compare celebration options with the right questions

A directory listing rarely answers every question for a group celebration. When you contact a restaurant, ask about the specific date, approximate guest count, current food and drink options, and any arrangements the group needs. If you want a reservation or a particular type of seating, ask whether it is available rather than assuming it is offered.

It also helps to distinguish between a general restaurant visit and an event package. A restaurant’s cuisine or atmosphere does not confirm private dining, decorations, a set menu, special pricing, or other celebration services. Ask directly about each requested detail and make your decision based on the answer.
