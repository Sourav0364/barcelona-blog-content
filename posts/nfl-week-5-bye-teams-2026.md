## What does a regular-season bye mean?

A bye is a scheduled week without a regular-season game for that team. The NFL’s 2026 regular season spans 18 weeks, with each team scheduled for 17 games and one bye week. A bye is part of the published schedule; it does not mean a Week 5 game was postponed or that the team has an extra game to make up.

For Week 5, that makes the Chiefs and Panthers the teams to leave off a list of that week’s scheduled games. The rest of the weekly slate still has its own matchups and kickoff details, so use the official NFL schedule for the game you want to follow rather than assuming the bye list covers the full slate.
