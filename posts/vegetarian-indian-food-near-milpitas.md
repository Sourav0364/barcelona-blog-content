## How to compare vegetarian options

Before choosing a restaurant, decide what “vegetarian” means for your group. Some diners avoid meat and seafood but consume dairy or eggs. Others require vegan food, separate preparation, or clear allergen information. A restaurant may be able to answer some questions but not others, and policies can change.

Ask the following before you go:

1. Which current dishes are vegetarian?
2. Do those dishes contain dairy, eggs, or other ingredients I avoid?
3. Are shared cooking surfaces or utensils used?
4. Can the kitchen explain the ingredients and preparation?
5. Are the dishes currently available at the time of my visit?
6. Can the restaurant accommodate a group with different dietary preferences?

Do not infer an answer from a dish name, a photograph, a cuisine label, or an older social post. If a dietary restriction is medically important, explain it clearly when calling and confirm whether the restaurant can safely accommodate it.
