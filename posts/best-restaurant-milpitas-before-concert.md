## Start with the show, then choose a restaurant

A pre-concert meal is time-sensitive. Begin with the event’s official information: confirm the venue address, doors-open time, and start time. Then check the route between the restaurant and the event location. Do not assume that a restaurant in Milpitas is close to a particular concert venue or that the event itself is in Milpitas.

Next, estimate how much time you can comfortably spend eating. A relaxed meal and a short stop have different requirements. Before choosing, confirm current operating hours, food availability at the time you plan to arrive, and any details that might affect your schedule. If you are going with others, agree on the meeting point and timing before you leave.

## Pre-show restaurant checklist

Use this quick list before you choose:

1. Confirm the concert venue’s exact address and event schedule from an official source.
2. Check the restaurant’s current hours and whether food is available at your intended time.
3. Ask about the current menu if you have a particular dish, dietary need, or food preference in mind.
4. Check the route from the restaurant to the venue instead of relying on a search label such as “near.”
5. Leave enough time for the meal and the trip to the event.
6. If a specific drink or other detail matters, confirm it directly rather than assuming it is available.
