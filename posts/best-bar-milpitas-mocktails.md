## Be clear about what you mean by “mocktail”

People may use “mocktail,” “zero-proof,” and “nonalcoholic cocktail” to mean an intentionally alcohol-free drink, but menus and preparation practices vary. If avoiding alcohol is important, say that directly and ask the restaurant to confirm the complete drink and how it is made. Do not assume that a drink labeled “low-ABV” or “spirit-free” meets your needs without checking.

When comparing Milpitas bars, consider these questions:

- **Is the drink completely alcohol-free?** Ask about every ingredient and any alcohol-containing bitters, mixers, or garnishes if that matters to you.
- **What is available now?** An old menu image or review may not reflect the current selection.
- **Can staff confirm the ingredients?** Mention any dietary or ingredient concern clearly; ask only what the business can verify.
- **Does the menu fit your group?** If others are ordering cocktails, confirm whether there are suitable alternatives rather than assuming a one-for-one substitute exists.
- **Does the location work?** Check the exact address and route, especially when a result is listed as “near Milpitas.”
