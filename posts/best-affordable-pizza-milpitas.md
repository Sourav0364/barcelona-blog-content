## Compare the complete order, not just one price

An affordable choice depends on what you need to feed and how you plan to get it. Before settling on a result, check the current menu and compare like with like:

- **Portion and size:** Confirm the listed size and how many people the order is intended to serve; do not rely on a photo alone.
- **What is included:** Check whether toppings or other add-ons change the price.
- **Total at checkout:** Review taxes, delivery or service fees, minimums, and any other charges before placing an order.
- **Current offers:** Verify the dates, conditions, and participating items for any advertised deal directly with the restaurant.
- **Pickup or delivery:** Confirm which service is actually available for your address and desired time.
- **Dietary needs:** Ask about ingredients and preparation directly if a food requirement matters.

These checks can make comparisons more useful than a general “cheap pizza” label, which may not reflect the order you have in mind.
