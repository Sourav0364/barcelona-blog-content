## How to make a better brunch search

Use a few practical filters instead of relying on a single “best brunch” list:

- **Timing:** Confirm when the restaurant begins serving and whether weekend service differs from weekdays.
- **Menu fit:** Ask about current dishes, dietary needs, and whether shareable options are available that day.
- **Atmosphere:** Decide whether you want a quiet meal, a lively bar, or a sports-focused setting.
- **Drinks:** Verify current cocktail, sangria, coffee, and other beverage options before making plans.
